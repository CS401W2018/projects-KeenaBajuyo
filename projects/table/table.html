About the Data

This table displays LeBron James' career statistics with the Lakers and overall career totals. LeBron started playing for the Los Angeles Lakers in 2018. Over his 8 years with the Lakers, he has definitely proven himself and contributed a lot to the team. His individual statistics show that he ranks 11th in 3-pointers, 25th in rebounds, 4th in assists, 6th in all-time steals, and is the all-time leading scorer in NBA history with 41,924 points. This includes his previous scores from his time with the Cleveland Cavaliers and Miami Heat.

The table identifies LeBron's solid performance across various seasons with the Lakers. For example, in the 2019-20 season, he had 148 three-pointers and 1,698 points. His ability to play at high levels season after season attests to his dedication and skills. The table also displays his work in a rebound, assist, block, and steal as a representative of his complete game.

Today, LeBron James is 22 years in the league, the oldest player in the league. Even in his late years, he remains a presence and a star of the Lakers, proving to everyone that his skill and energy are ageless and he can never tire playing the game. His capability of keeping himself at his best physique and adjusting the way he plays has kept him competitive at the top level.
