Ernie Sabellano Bajuyo

He is the third of eight siblings, born and raised in Cagayan de Oro, Philippines. He was a basketball varsity player from high school to college, which provided him with a scholarship until he graduated with a degree in Criminology. He then served a mission in the Philippines Davao Mission, where he met my mother. After completing his mission, he returned for her. He then applied to the Philippine National Police Academy and started working there at 28. He has been working there for 35 years and is now a Police Lieutenant Colonel. He plans to retire this year and start a business in Manila, Philippines.

Marites Remulta Bajuyo

She is the fourth of eight children, born and raised in Davao City, Philippines. Despite being the fourth child, she acted like an older sister to everyone because of her charisma. She graduated with a degree in Medical Technology and found a job as a Medical Technologist at Southeastern Medical Center, Davao City Branch. She was then called as the Stake Primary President for Davao Philippines Stake, serving for 10 years, the longest term on record. She retired at 55 and is now busy with her own business, making and selling the best Embutido in Davao City.
