The Title and Topic of My Site.

Title: Explore History: Virtual Tour of Fort Santiago, Intramuros.

Topic: I have decided on is a virtual tour of one of the most historical sites in the Philippines, which is found in Intramuros, Manila, specifically Fort Santiago.

Why I Chose This Topic.

Ever since I was a kid, I've always dreamed of visiting Fort Santiago because I've admired our National Hero, Jose Rizal. This is the exact place where he spent his last days. Since Fort Santiago is located far away from where I live, I've always just looked online for videos and images and continued to save money so that I can go. Fort Santiago is also one of the most historical sites in the Philippines, serving as the main headquarters for the three foreign countries (Spain, America, and Japan) that colonized us for 384 years before we gained our sovereignty. Before coming here, I had the chance to visit this place and learn more about the different sites and their historical significance. Up until today, I continue to study and learn more about my country's history. I chose this topic because I want to showcase what I have learned and allow my fellow classmates to virtually experience the beauty of Fort Santiago.
