Fort Santiago

Fort Santiago is a fortress in Intramuros, Manila, Philippines. It was built by the Spanish colonial administration and was also named after Saint James (Santiago in Spanish). The fort was significant to Philippine history since it was both a military defense complex and a political prison that housed political detainees, one of whom was national hero José Rizal.

Read more about Fort Santiago

Fort Santiago is a popular tourist destination today, proudly showcasing its history and gardens. Visitors may stroll along the fort walls, dungeons, and museums that tell the story of the nation's colonial history.
