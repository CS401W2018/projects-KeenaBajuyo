Fort Santiago

Fort Santiago is a citadel located in Intramuros, Manila, Philippines. It was built by the Spanish colonial government and is named after Saint James (Santiago in Spanish). The fort played a significant role in the history of the Philippines, serving as a military defense structure and a prison for political prisoners, including the national hero José Rizal.

Today, Fort Santiago is a popular tourist destination, showcasing its historical significance and beautiful gardens. Visitors can explore the fort's walls, dungeons, and museums that highlight the country's colonial past.

This page offers a virtual tour of Fort Santiago, providing insights into its historical significance and key sights. Discover the rich history and cultural heritage of this iconic fortress.
