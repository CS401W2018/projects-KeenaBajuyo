Dungeons of Fort Santiago

Dungeons of Fort Santiago are a series of underground chambers and tunnels that served as prisons and storage areas during the Spanish colonial period. These dungeons were used to hold prisoners, including political detainees, and were often dark, damp, and cramped. The dungeons are a significant part of Fort Santiago's history, as they reflect the harsh conditions faced by those imprisoned there.

Today, the dungeons are part of the Fort Santiago complex and are open to visitors. They provide a glimpse into the past and serve as a reminder of the fort's role in Philippine history.

The White Cross

The White Cross is a memorial monument located within Fort Santiago that commemorates the Filipino and American soldiers who lost their lives and died while they were engaged in combat during World War II. The cross symbolizes the bravery of these heroic men for their country. It reminds one of the strength and courage of the people of the Philippines during a stormy period in the past.

The White Cross is a significant location within Fort Santiago, attracting visitors who wish to pay tribute and learn more about sacrifices made during war. The memorial is overshadowed by beautiful gardens, providing a peaceful space for reflection.
