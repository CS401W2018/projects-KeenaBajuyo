Engagement - Form

The form tag has an "action" attribute. What does it do?

The "action" attribute specifies where to send the form-data when a form is submitted.

The form tag has a "method" attribute. What does it do?

The "method" attribute specifies the HTTP method (GET or POST) to be used when sending form-data.

What is the difference between a "POST" and a "GET"?

GET requests data from a specified resource, while POST submits data to be processed to a specified resource.

Engagement - Form Styling

What are different ways to style your form?

Some ways to style your form are by using CSS, Bootstrap, or JavaScript. However, in our class, the elements mostly used were fieldset, form, and legend.

What is the difference between "padding" and "margin"?

Padding is the space inside the border, while margin is the space outside the border.
